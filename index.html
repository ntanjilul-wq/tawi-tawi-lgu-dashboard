How to use this dashboard (30 seconds)
  1. Overview: switch the census year, then filter by income class or search. Click any map marker or bar to open that municipality's profile.

  3. Other Surveys: adds PSA poverty (FIES and small-area estimates) and Labor Force Survey indicators.
  4. Compare: sets two municipalities side by side with a radar chart.
  5. Barangays: lists the 20 largest barangays in the province.

Notes on the Data

  • 2024 population and income class: PSA PSGC page for Tawi-Tawi (total 482,645). 2020 population: PSA 2020 Census of Population and Housing (total 440,276).
  • Growth rates and "people added" are computed by this dashboard from those PSA counts.
  • 2015 population is shown only for the 8 municipalities where the PSA release figure was retrieved; other cells say "Not in dataset". Area (2013), 2020 density, barangay count and 2015-2020 growth are likewise shown only where available.
  • The barangay ranking uses 2020 CPH counts compiled from PSA data by Philatlas. Please confirm exact figures on OpenSTAT.
  • Provincial poverty uses first-semester FIES figures (2018, 2021, 2023) with 90% confidence intervals from the PSA report; national figures are from the same table. Compare families with families only.
  • Municipal poverty (2023 small area estimates, share of population) was read from municipality pages that cite the PSA release, so please confirm each value on PSA or OpenSTAT. Mapun and Turtle Islands are shown as "Not in dataset" because a 2023 value was not retrieved.
  • Labor Force Survey figures are 2025 preliminary provincial estimates and may be revised.
  • Income classes are as currently listed by PSA and may differ from older tables.
